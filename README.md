> Empowering common people to understand legal documents in simple language.

### 🎯 Problem
85% of Indians cannot understand legal documents due to complex English. This causes fraud and injustice.

### 💡 Solution
LegalEase AI uses Generative AI to:
- Explain legal documents in Tamil, Hindi & simple English
- Generate Rent Agreement, Affidavit, NDA instantly
- Chat with your legal document like ChatGPT

### 🚀 Features
- 📄 AI Document Explainer
- 📝 Auto Legal Document Generator
- 💬 Legal Chatbot (RAG)
- 🌐 Multi-language: English, Tamil, Hindi
- ⚡ Built with FastAPI + OpenAI

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- Backend: Python, FastAPI
- AI: OpenAI GPT, LangChain
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
